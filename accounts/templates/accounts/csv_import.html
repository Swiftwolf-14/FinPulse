Required CSV columns

Your CSV should contain these column headings:

transaction_date, branch_code, transaction_type, reference_number, description, category, amount, account_name, related_reference

Supported transaction types

  • sale — records a sale.
  • payment_received — records money received.
  • expense — records an expense.
  • payment_made — records money paid out.

Uploading validates the CSV. It does not automatically post financial transactions.
